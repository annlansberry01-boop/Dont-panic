### Political actions: not bullet-listed in the book

The book describes the political focus for each chapter in a paragraph, not as a bullet list. For example:
- Transport: "Political agitation is about infrastructure. We need to build the electric trains, buses, bike lanes and safe walking infrastructure."

- Stuff: "Political agitation is about changing laws to support the end of landfill, nowadays called a 'circular economy'."
- Nature: "Political agitation is about campaigning to protect, enhance and restore nature at the national level."
- Food: "Political agitation is about changing the food system to care for the planet."
- Money: (not explicitly stated but implied — fossil fuel divestment, fair economy)

The political actions currently in the app are reasonable interpretations of this — not directly from the book but consistent with it.
